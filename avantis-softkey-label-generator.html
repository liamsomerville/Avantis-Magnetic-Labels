Print & fit

Critical: print at 100% / Actual size. Turn OFF "Fit to page" / "Shrink to fit" / any scaling, or the mm will be wrong.

  • Measure the 100mm reference line on the printout. If it isn't exactly 100mm, the printer is scaling — fix it and reprint.
  • Register each strip on two points: top edge to the top of its first key, bottom edge to the bottom of its last key.
  • Layer and pair strips use identical key height and gap, so they cut and register the same way.
  • The grey outline is the cut line for the 17mm strip.
  • Prints 1:1 on A4 or US Letter. The document declares no paper size, so the driver has nothing to rescale. The A4 / Letter switch changes the on-screen preview only.
